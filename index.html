PayFlow

Payment processing & settlement engine
Live

A TypeScript, Express and PostgreSQL payments API covering idempotent creation, capture, settlement, partial and full refunds, HMAC-signed webhooks and an audit trail. Idempotency keys backed by a unique index plus SELECT … FOR UPDATE row locks stop double charges and over-refunds under concurrent requests; the 2% fee and 18% GST math lives in a pure, unit-tested service.
